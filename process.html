Our Process

A step-by-step roadmap to legally clear your debt review status and restore your financial freedom.

Speak to a Consultant

How It Works

Step 1

Initial Consultation

We perform a free assessment of your credit profile and debt review status to determine your eligibility for removal and gather the necessary documents.

Step 2

Legal & NCR Audit

Our specialists review your existing debt review status, credit provider records, and court documents to establish the correct legal pathway for removal.

Step 3

Clearance or Court Order

Depending on your scenario, we issue a Form 19 Clearance Certificate (if settled) or proceed with a Court Order Rescission (if wrongly listed).

Step 4

Credit Bureau Notification

We submit all signed legal documentation and clearance certificates to all registered South African credit bureaus (TransUnion, Experian, XDS, Compuscan).

Step 5

Profile Clearance

The credit bureaus update their systems, removing the debt review flag from your profile. You are provided with an updated clearance confirmation.

Two Pathways to Freedom

Pathway A: Clearance Certificate

Pathway B: Court Order Rescission

Ideal for clients who were fraudulently, erroneously, or wrongfully placed under debt review[cite: 1]:

  • Draft an affidavit detailing the circumstances of the wrongful listing[cite: 1].
  • File an application in the Magistrate's Court[cite: 1].

  • Instruct credit bureaus to permanently remove the flag[cite: 1].
