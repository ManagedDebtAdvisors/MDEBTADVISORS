Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries regarding debt review and our advisory services.

Debt review is a legal process regulated by the National Credit Act (NCA) designed to assist over-indebted consumers by restructuring their monthly debt payments into one affordable consolidated monthly installment.

We evaluate your budget, calculate an affordable monthly repayment amount, and negotiate reduced interest rates and lower installments directly with your credit providers.

Once you are placed under debt review, credit providers are legally barred from contacting you directly or taking legal action against you regarding the debts included in the process.
