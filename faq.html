Debt Review Removal FAQ

Find answers to common questions about removing debt review flags and clearing your credit record.

Yes. You can be removed from debt review if all accounts included in your debt restructuring plan have been fully paid off, or if you can demonstrate that you were incorrectly placed under debt review without proper consent or a court order.

A Form 19 Clearance Certificate is an official document issued by a registered Debt Counsellor confirming that all your debt review obligations have been met. This certificate is submitted to all credit bureaus to remove the debt review indicator from your profile.

Once the Clearance Certificate (Form 19) or rescission order is submitted to the major credit bureaus (Experian, TransUnion, XDS, Compuscan), it typically takes 5 to 21 working days for your credit status to reflect as fully cleared.

If you were fraudulently or mistakenly registered under debt review without signing agreement documents, we can initiate a dispute and legal investigation process to have the flag legally declared invalid and removed.
